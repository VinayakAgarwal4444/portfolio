Multi-agent path planning for railway networks

Research project  ·  Jul to Nov 2024

Trains sharing track cannot simply take the shortest route each. I implemented A* with a Manhattan heuristic for the single agent case, then safe interval path planning and prioritised planning to coordinate many agents through dynamic obstacles, timing constraints and malfunctions. Priority-based search and large neighbourhood search handled conflict resolution at scale.
